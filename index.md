## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Researcher at the **Multi-modal Interaction Center, [Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI)](https://www.baai.ac.cn/en/)**, where I lead the research on **long-horizon task planning for humanoid robots** (specifically the Unitree G1 project). 

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the **[Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS)](https://english.ict.cas.cn/)** in 2025, supervised by **[Prof. Ruiping Wang](https://rpwang.net/)** and **[Prof. Xilin Chen](https://vipl.ict.ac.cn/people/_xlchen/)**. Before that, I earned my B.S. in Electronic Information Engineering from **Xidian University**.


## 🔍 Research Interests

- **Embodied AI:** Long-horizon task planning, Vision-Language Navigation (VLN), humanoid robot teleoperation and control.
- **Multi-modal Learning:** Video Question Answering (VideoQA), Egocentric video understanding, Multi-modal foundation models.
